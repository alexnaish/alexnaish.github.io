# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are hiring managers and recruiters assessing Alex Naish for software engineering roles. They need to understand his expertise and decide whether to contact him.

## Product Purpose

naish.dev demonstrates Alex's engineering expertise in a modest, non-pushy way. Success means a relevant hiring manager can quickly understand his experience and continue the conversation through LinkedIn or email.

## Positioning

Alex is a full stack software engineer with lead and tech lead experience. His current technical focus is TypeScript and Node.js.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate Alex's professional fit on the public web, then use LinkedIn or email to contact him. Alex is based in the United Kingdom.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The product is a personal professional website at `naish.dev`.
- Alex's public role is "Software Engineer."
- LinkedIn (`https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexnaish/`) and email (`alex@naish.dev`) are the required contact routes.
- The site must not overstate expertise, experience, availability, or evidence.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the name "Alex Naish."
- Communicate expertise with a modest, non-pushy voice.
- Preserve full stack, lead, and tech lead experience as core professional context.

## Evidence on Hand

- The existing homepage names Alex Naish and presents software, full stack, web, JavaScript, Node.js, React, MongoDB, and HTML5 engineering terms in `index.html`.
- No case studies, project evidence, testimonials, employment history, client names, performance claims, or availability claims are present. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make professional fit quick to assess.
- Let specific expertise carry the message instead of promotional claims.
- Keep every experience and technology claim factual.
- Make direct professional contact easy.
